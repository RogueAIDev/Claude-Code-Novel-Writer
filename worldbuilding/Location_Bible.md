## ***ENGLAND — NORFOLK***

### ***Sterling Castle, Norfolk***

*The ancestral seat of the Duke of Norwich. A fortified castle with battlements of quarried stone, a great square keep, a chapel spire, and a long eastern range. The castle stands on a plateau above protected forest. In a storm the west wing moans; the battlements are unmoved by what the weather throws against them.*

***Key interiors and areas:***

*The Duke's private study. Edward's working room: a broad oak desk, the smell of tobacco and cold pipe ash, a fire, drawn curtains against the wet dusk. "For Crown and Kin" is carved into the stone above the hearth. A three-note brass bell summons staff. Edward holds a cabinet set into the wall, unlocked by a key worn on a chain against his skin. The study is where Ewan Macrae gives his account of Loch Maree, where the mission is revealed, where Alexander learns the truth of his family's work, where Niall lights a fire to have something ordinary to do with his hands, and where Alexander winds his father's watch on the evening of 13 January 1853\. Inheritance Chapters 2, 4, 26\.*

*The records room adjoining the study. Niall's workspace during the prequel: candlelit, cold, with ledgers and an older catalogue of Gaelic place names. He is working here when the bell summons him on the night of the storm.*

*The great hall. Candlelit, with the long table and harvest decorations. The festival dinner, including Catherine's defining line and Hessian's first appearance at table. Inheritance Chapter 1\.*

*The great kitchen and morning room. Mrs. Thompson presides over the kitchen. Catherine is perched on the great table with a tart on her knee and flour on her cuff in the book's second scene. The morning room is where Pike is received and where the public account of Edward and Amelia's deaths is given. Inheritance Chapters 1, 3, 25\.*

*The long gallery. Portraits, tall uncurtained windows. Hessian's first private exchange with Catherine — "That sounds less like a life than a waiting room" — takes place here near midnight. Inheritance Chapter 1\.*

*The kitchen garden and its low wall. Niall sees something at the garden wall just before midnight and goes after it without breaking stride. The creature is Calum in raptor form, scouting the house. Later, the boggart child is found in the hedge beyond the garden. Inheritance Chapters 1, 12\.*

*The festival yard. The yard by the gate: cider cart, judging table, tenants, estate hands. The Harvest Festival opens here. The rooks lift from the chestnut trees when Hessian's carriage arrives and do not settle until the wheels are out of hearing. Inheritance Chapter 1\.*

*The front steps and carriage sweep. Where Hessian arrives at dusk on an overcast evening. Inheritance Chapter 1\.*

*The lower training yard. The butt of the old chestnut is used as a target; a bench stands against the wall. The first knife lesson between Niall and Alexander takes place here at the story's close in the prequel, and the lessons have continued for six years when Inheritance opens. The phrase "Tomorrow, then" originates here. Alexander trains here on live steel for the first time in Chapter 6 of Inheritance, and returns to the yard in the final scene of the book, when he asks Niall to stand beside him. Prequel, Chapter 7; Inheritance Chapters 1, 6, 26\.*

*The upper gallery. A passage from which Niall glimpses a lit window and Alexander bent over a book on the night of the storm. The young duke has observed Niall from here twice before they meet. Alexander watches the return from Scotland from the upper gallery as well. Prequel Chapters 1, 7\.*

*The east corridor and Catherine's room. Catherine's room is in the east wing, with balcony doors. The locked door, the open balcony, and the gouges in the plaster mark her departure. The jewellery box with a false bottom holds her diaries and pin-money jar; the atlas is missing. After the return, the atlas is back on the table, salt-stained, and Catherine plans a route she means to take for herself. Inheritance Chapters 2, 3, 25\.*

*The castle battlements and the corridor outside the study. Where Alexander goes after Hessian's departure to challenge his father about the study scene. Inheritance Chapter 2\.*

*The library. Where Alexander asks Niall in February 1852 whether he believes Catherine is alive: the only honest conversation either has had in four months. Inheritance Chapter 3\.*

*The gatehouse. The bell rings in a pattern that is neither fire nor thieves. Ewan arrives through the storm just before midnight on 30 November 1852\. Inheritance Chapter 4\.*

*The undercroft passage and the iron-strapped door. An iron-strapped door in the undercroft passage that Alexander has been kept from all his life. It opens onto a stair that descends through centuries of masonry, each layer plainer and older than the last. Niall refuses to open it without Edward's order in Chapter 4; Alexander descends through it in Chapter 5\. Beyond it lies the prequel's machinery, the council chamber, and everything beneath the keep. The door in the prequel is not yet opened on the page but is identified as the same door. Prequel (referenced); Inheritance Chapters 4, 5\.*

*The council chamber beneath the keep. A long table scarred by a century of tea rings and ink. Lady Beatrice Vale presides. Sebastian Thorne stands by the door. The institution is revealed to Alexander here. Two chairs stand empty after the deaths. Alexander speaks the oath, and Niall answers, at the book's close. Inheritance Chapters 5, 26\.*

*The sanctuary beneath the keep. Beyond the council chamber, a vault lit gold by hanging lamps — a different quality of light from the cold blue of the corridors. Pallets line one wall. Two boggart families from the fen country are sheltered here. Mrs. Ashworth keeps their accounts in the same ledger style as the linen counts. The boggart child bolts past her and out into the snow in Chapter 12\. Calum sits here among damaged books with a silver scar over his heart in Chapter 25\. Inheritance Chapters 5, 12, 25\.*

*The hidden infirmary. A room of steel tables and gas lamps turned up too bright for comfort. Dr. Harcourt's domain. She examines the contents of Ewan's oilcloth parcel — Catherine's blood on a painter's brush and Ewan's copy of the swallow — here. The cure search begins at this table. Inheritance Chapters 6, 12, 25\.*

*Beatrice's records room. Shelves of personnel files going back a century. Alexander finds Samuel Pryce's name here: the Sterling butler who spied for Ashcroft and was dismissed in 1841\. Inheritance Chapter 5\.*

*The armory. Where Edward's reserve sword (plain, silver-etched) and a case of silver-edged throwing knives are kept. Alexander adds the blunt practice knife and Catherine's swallow handkerchief to his field kit here. Inheritance Chapter 6\.*

*The vault beneath the stables. Older than the horses above it. Gideon Wren's domain when he visits: the smuggler's table, the stamped metal token, the forged manifests. Gideon refuses payment here after the second assault. Inheritance Chapters 6, 25\.*

*The sanctuary library alcove. Where Calum sits with damaged books and the new silver scar; Niall laughs in Old Town Scots. Inheritance Chapter 25\.*

*The western service passage and restricted storerooms. The covered wagons from Loch Maree are taken here through the western passage on the party's return; O'Brien directs the footmen while Alexander watches from the upper gallery. Prequel Chapter 7\.*

***Grounds and estate:***

*The drive and the gap in the estate wall. Pike watches from the drive as wagons leave in Chapter 6\. A gap in the estate wall that appears on no map is where Gideon waits with a shuttered lantern when Alexander leaves Sterling land. Past the second mile, the wards fall away — no gate, only a change in the air.*

*Harrow's Fen. A water-meadow on the Sterling estate feeding eleven families in winter of 1851\. The Duke will not sell it. Hessian offers a large sum for it but is refused. It becomes the symbol of Sterling stewardship. Inheritance Chapters 1, 2\.*

*The fen-side wall. A wall on the fen side where the ward has thinned. An Institute watcher tests its whole length while the family is away in December 1852, leaving a gentleman's boot prints. Alexander asks Thomas to be steward and instructs him about the wall in Chapter 25\. Inheritance Chapters 12, 25\.*

***Atmosphere.** Storm-lashed in the prequel; the castle held against the weather while everything inside it was set in motion. The courtyard rings with bells when the household is roused. The west wing moans in wind. Generations of secrecy are pressed into the walls, and the children can feel what they are not being told.*

***Continuity note.** The private stair Amelia uses, the butler's pantry door Alexander passes without looking at, and Niall's back-stair to the armory are all named in passing and should be consistent if they recur.*

### ***Seraphina's Cottage, Norfolk***

*Two miles from Sterling Castle, on foot across country (Amelia walks it rather than calling the carriage, because some conversations need the weather scoured off first). Herbs hang from the beams; a scrying basin sits on the table. In Inheritance Chapter 2, Amelia brings Edward's hand-copied deed here, and Seraphina lays it across the basin: where the water touches Hessian's signature, the ink darkens rather than running. The thin silver-grey lines Loch Maree left in Amelia's palms catch the lamplight. Seraphina tells Amelia something precise and unsatisfying, and Amelia notices. The council chamber annex during the escape planning also uses a scrying basin; this seems to be a different space rather than the cottage. Inheritance Chapters 2, 6\.*

## ***ENGLAND — LONDON***

### ***The Sterling Townhouse, London***

*A townhouse in a London square. In November 1852 the city is in black for Wellington's funeral: coal fog, black crape in shop windows, early dark. Lady Aldermere and Mrs. Fenhallow call and mention the Ashcroft Institute's new lecture season. A sighting letter arrives that night; Edward breaks the seal standing, which he never does, and by midnight he and Amelia have gone north. Alexander watches the empty square and understands that protection and exclusion feel exactly the same. Key spaces: the drawing room, the front hall, the steps. Inheritance Chapter 3\.*

---

### ***The Ashcroft Institute, Mayfair***

***Public face.** A respectable Mayfair house in a London square with black railings polished by rain, pale stone, curtained windows, and a brass plate reading "Ashcroft Institute For Natural Inquiry." Members of Parliament have attended lectures there; physicians have praised its collections. The emblem on all Institute equipment is an open eye above a cage. Prequel Chapter 6\.*

***The private upper corridor.** Reached beyond the public galleries; Percival crosses it with one hand pressed to the bandage at his throat after Loch Maree. Two iron doors at the corridor's end lead into Silas's laboratory. Prequel Chapter 6\.*

***Silas Ashcroft's private laboratory.** Warmer than the rest of the house. Gas jets hiss behind wire guards. Glass cases climb floor to ceiling holding preserved wings, horns, bones, skins, and organs suspended in amber fluid. A row of experimental galvanic cells lines one wall, copper and zinc plates each labeled in a meticulous hand; fine wires run from them to a restraint collar fixed around a carved wooden throat. A galvanic relay clicks behind the wall. On Silas's desk: a file on Alasdair Fraser (written by the former field director), a packet of newspaper clippings, a social register, and a recent engraved portrait of the Sterling family. Percival's scar beneath his jaw is examined here. Silas sets out his long strategy — observation, cultivation, questions rather than accusations — in this room. Prequel Chapter 6; Series Bible.*

## ***THE ROAD NORTH***

### ***A Goods Siding in the Fens***

*Where Alexander, Niall, Ewan, and Gideon board between one shunting engine and the next. Gideon forges manifests on a crate lid. Davey Coop, a crate-marking boy, runs errands. Raw noon under flat grey cloud, wind off the fen. Inheritance Chapter 7\.*

### ***The Drovers' Van (the Northern Freight Line)***

*A slow freight train heading north: a stove, tobacco smoke, the smell of cattle. Tam Rennick harasses an old woman; Alexander turns the van against him without raising his voice. Ewan, settled against the planking, asks whether they'll see Rhoswen and uses her name without noticing. Inheritance Chapter 7\.*

### ***A Junction Platform at York***

*Where the party must change trains. A man in a coat too good for a goods yard marks Niall across the platform and turns away a moment too late. Gideon reroutes them over the Cheviots within the hour. Inheritance Chapter 7\.*

### ***A Hotel near the Perth Railhead***

*Antagonist interlude. Percival reads a telegram from the Institute watcher, his father's latest letter open beside it. The letter says observe, record, don't engage. Percival touches the scar beneath his jaw. He decides to observe at close range. The Scottish rail line ends at Perth; from here the road goes by coach and horse. Inheritance Chapter 7\.*

### ***Webb's Inn, Alwinton, Northumberland***

*A drovers' inn at the foot of Clennell Street, a few miles from the English-Scottish border. Josiah Webb, the innkeeper, knew Ewan's grandfather from the kelp trade. Snow is beginning, the Cheviots dark above. By the fire, Niall gives Alexander the warmer side and takes the draught himself, a habit so old neither notices. The inn burns in the early hours of Monday 6 December 1852; Catherine's diary burns with it. Key spaces: the inn yard, the taproom, the foot of the stair, the narrow stair itself, the stable, and the upper floor. Inheritance Chapters 7–8.*

### ***Clennell Street Drove Road, Cheviot Hills***

*The old drove road over the Cheviots, climbing out of England into snow. The old track the cattle took for centuries. The border ridge: at the top, Ewan points north where the hills fold away toward Hawick. Alexander looks back once at the smoke and does not look back again. Sun clears the ridge at about 8:20; deep snow on the high ground, wind. Inheritance Chapter 8\.*

### ***The Drumochter Road and Pass, Grampian Mountains***

*The military road north over Drumochter after the railway ends at Perth. Two days of hard riding in snow. A rise above the road is where Ewan first spots the Institute convoy — three wagons, a covered cage, men in good coats and worse boots, the hiss of galvanic cells in waxed-canvas housings. Inheritance Chapter 9\.*

### ***Dalwhinnie Ford***

*A ford and toll point on the Drumochter road. Gideon's forged papers stop the Institute convoy here as a customs matter. Niall cuts the silver mesh; the kelpie foal screams. A wagon slews on the ice and Ewan's leg breaks under a wheel. Percival Ashcroft arrives on horseback, absurdly overdressed for the mountains, silver-headed cane across his saddle. He says "Sterling" as though naming a minor inconvenience. Inheritance Chapter 9\.*

### ***A Roadside Camp in a Pine Shelter***

*One night's camp after the ford, while the company cannot yet push the wounded and burned foal any faster. Niall tells Alexander that Edward would have done the same thing; Alexander asks whether he truly would, and does not know. Inheritance Chapter 9\.*

### ***The Black Water Gorge, Ross-shire***

*West of Inverness. A narrow timber bridge crosses a gorge half-sealed by ice; the bridge has been cut. Beag goes into the black water after Ewan and brings him out downstream. The birch wood on the far bank is where Calum walks out of the trees, carrying a birch-bark message. He and Niall exchange "Nillo" and "Colie." The birch bark carries a swallow in blood and a mark Ewan reads as the head of Loch Maree. The chapter's lowest point for the central relationship: Alexander's trust is broken a second time when Niall admits he knew Calum all along. Snow is beginning; the water is black with ice on the edges. Inheritance Chapter 10\.*

### ***The Road West through Ross-shire***

*The ride west toward Loch Maree after the gorge. A silence that has a shape. Alexander checks the dressing on Niall's arm at nightfall without a word. Inheritance Chapter 10\.*

## ***SCOTLAND — LOCH MAREE AND THE NORTH***

### ***Loch Maree, Scottish Highlands***

*A large, deep freshwater loch in Wester Ross, surrounded by mountains. The prequel calls it "immense and dark, its surface flattened by the cold until it resembled a sheet of hammered iron." Mountain slopes rise in blackening tiers; peaks vanish into coming night. Strands of fog move over the water like pale fingers. The loch goes quiet in a way that is not merely absence of sound — the horses press together, ears toward the water; no birds call; even a joke will not take. Mid-December sunrise at approximately 8:40, sunset at approximately 3:13, giving just over six hours of daylight: all vampire scenes have been placed accordingly.*

*The loch is sacred ground in the deepest sense. It is the site of the founding covenant between Macbeth and Eithne, a kelpie queen who is Rhoswen's foremother. A covenant marking cut into the rock of the cave tunnel belongs to this family of sigils. The Vanguard places no claim on the loch or its inhabitants; their help is always chosen, never commanded.*

***Key features and sub-locations:***

*The eastern shore and village. Ewan Macrae's home. Cottages, some with black cloth across the doors for the dead. The village blames the kelpie — called "Rhos" locally — for the deaths. The shingle shore here is where Beag stops on the return journey in Inheritance and Rhoswen rises to meet her foal. The mercy debt is opened here. Prequel; Inheritance Chapter 11\.*

*The reeds and shallows. Where Morag Campbell drowned: crushed reeds, churned mud, two straight grooves through the mud from the Ashcroft apparatus, dark stains on the stones, the torn edge of a shoe strap in the mesh. Prequel.*

*The northern shore and cliffs. The trail of blood and boot prints leads here; the land folds inward, the loch's voice changes, waves strike pockets hidden under rock. Heavy boots from the Institute, the narrow heel of a gentleman's riding boot, two parallel ruts from a transport cart.*

*The cave entrance. Barely more than a split in the cliff face, half-swallowed by fallen stone and curtains of black weeds. Water breathes in and out across the threshold. Smell: brine, rust, scorched metal, something sweetly rotten. The passage slopes down, water running in a thin sheet across the floor, the ceiling low enough to bow Edward's head. The flat ground east of this entrance is the Rowan Circle massacre site; see the separate entry below. Prequel.*

*The inner cavern. A surprisingly large vaulted chamber with columns of black stone slick with moisture. A broad tidal pool fills most of the floor, its surface broken by submerged rock and the dark geometry of chains. Along the far wall, a recent collapse seals what was once a larger passage; black water still moves beneath the rubble. Along the far wall: pitch torches, Ashcroft winches and galvanic batteries bolted into stone, the silver-threaded mesh and copper-lined winches. Ten mercenaries on the floor, an eleventh by a pillar; one field clerk with his ledger. The ledge above provides a vantage. Prequel.*

*The old carving in the tunnel. A shape cut into the rock wall, old enough that the stone has wept lime over half of it. Matches nothing in Niall's training. Edward's gaze catches on it; Amelia does not turn her head. It belongs to the covenant's family of markings and matches the sigils in the refuge relief. Prequel.*

*The two standing stones at the channel mouth. Two worn standing stones at the western end of the loch, at the mouth of a drowned channel. Rhoswen's crossing to the Vanguard camp begins here. She will carry the party once, without metal — all blades, buckles, and hardware must be left on the bank. The same stones are the staging point before the one-way passage into the refuge. Inheritance Chapters 11, 14\.*

*The loch's edge and shingle. Where Amelia reads the birch bark and the water remembers. Where Niall tells Alexander everything before he has to ask it. Where Alexander sits on Pinch Point Two with the signet unworn. Where Niall comes to sit beside him. Where the notebook is written in Gaelic. Where the party gathers at dusk before entering the channel. Where Rhoswen lays Calum in the shallows at dawn, and Beag comes out to stand beside her. Inheritance Chapters 13, 14, 19, 24\.*

*The ward line of standing pines. Half a mile north of the Vanguard camp; old protective work has discolored the bark. Calum waits just outside the line and gives what the Blood-Mark allows: patrol times, the thrall count, the location of the ritual chamber. Inheritance Chapter 12\.*

*The Vanguard camp. On the far shore of the loch, reached by Rhoswen's crossing. Canvas tents, a fire, the command tent, the map tent, the watch line on the perimeter. Beatrice's party arrives here overland from Poolewe. Inheritance Chapters 11–13.*

*The loch cave (after the climax). A cave at the loch shore where Rhoswen brings Catherine out of the sun after the refuge falls. Dim inside; sunlit water at the mouth. Catherine tells Alexander the pull is gone quiet. Inheritance Chapter 24\.*

*The western shore, by the channel mouth. Where the party gathers at dusk on 19 December. Edward removes his own coat and puts it on Alexander. The drowned channel begins here. Inheritance Chapter 14\.*

*The drowned channel. The passage under the hill from the western loch shore to the refuge. Walls of held water standing back on either side, green light filtered through a loch that should be over their heads, enormous cold. The way closes behind the party — the crossing can be made only once. Inheritance Chapter 14\.*

*Poolewe. The landing on the coast of Wester Ross where Beatrice's party comes ashore from the steamer, delayed by gales. A few miles from the loch's head. Inheritance Chapter 12\.*

*The loch where it pours into the drowned refuge (after the fall). After the flood, the loch runs into the refuge's lower halls. Alexander kneels here with his hands open and asks Rhoswen to hold the deep ways. Inheritance Chapter 21\.*

***Continuity note.** The seven-sigil marking in the cave tunnel of the prequel is the same family as the relief in the refuge. Edward commits it to memory; Niall files it away without understanding it. Percival also sees it but does not comment in the prequel. This is the seed for the sigil thread across the series.*

### ***The Northern Refuge (under the coastal tract)***

*An ancient refuge beneath the coastal tract that Edward sold to Hessian in 1851\. Its true builders were not human; its geometry almost repeats and does not. The Vanguard never knew it lay under the land Edward inherited with the title and never used. Hessian believes his ancestors built it; they built the fortress above the Iron Gates covenant, and he has moved the story north over centuries. The manor above is Hessian's.*

***Key interiors:***

*The vaulted threshold. Where the drowned channel ends. Behind them the water folds together without a sound. Amelia feels the covenant move through her palm on the stone. Alexander feels, for the first time, something in the stone that answers to him — a pressure like a hand on his shoulder — and tells no one. Inheritance Chapter 14\.*

*The refuge corridors and unmirrored wing. False wings that almost repeat but not quite: one carving in each is missing its twin. Alexander catches the second false turning before it costs them more than a held breath. Thrall patrols cross close enough overhead that Alexander feels their tread through his boot soles. Inheritance Chapter 15\.*

*The lower gallery with the cages. Iron cages holding humans taken from the glens: a drover, a shepherd's widow, a boy of perhaps twelve, all with bandaged arms. Gideon stays with the captives, promising to bring them out by whatever road opens. The bandaged arms explain what Catherine has been drinking. Inheritance Chapter 15\.*

*The round hall with the relief. Older than anything above it. A carved relief covers one wall: a crowned human figure stands beside a figure not human, their hands on the same staff. Beneath them, seven linked sigils in a line. Niall stops dead — the family of the carving is the same as the one in the Loch Maree cave tunnel. Seraphina reads the sigils by touch with her good eye useless in the dark. "One of seven. There were seven of these, once." Percival's surveyor makes rubbings of one line of sigils here. Inheritance Chapters 15, 17, 22\.*

*Catherine's room. Fine linen, a narrow bed, a looking-glass turned to the wall. The atlas is open on the table with a route inked across a map of the refuge's lower ways, drawn by Calum. Alexander cannot move at the threshold until Niall puts a hand on his back. Inheritance Chapters 3 (from Catherine's POV), 15\.*

*The junction of the servants' way. Where Seraphina holds a collapsing vault alone, pressing both hands to cracked stone, while the others go ahead to reach Catherine. She holds it long enough at the cost of everything left in her. Inheritance Chapter 15\.*

*The antechamber off the covenant chamber. A small room off the covenant chamber where Catherine stands waiting because Calum told her they would come. The family's reunion. Catherine tells them the ritual is tonight and that she will refuse Hessian in the chamber itself. Amelia tells her, gently, about the cages. Inheritance Chapter 15\.*

*The covenant chamber. A large chamber with a dais, an ancient seal in the floor, and a gallery above. The wards are tied to the covenant. The chamber answers to Sterling blood and to the purpose for which authority is claimed. The ritual is set here. Hessian has let them come — he wants Catherine's yes given in front of her family. The chamber is also the site of Alexander's claim: palm flat on the stone, signet over his heart, protection rather than possession. "By the authority this place recognizes, I revoke your welcome. You are named an enemy of Crown and Kin." The wards turn against Hessian and burn him wherever he touches the walls. Inheritance Chapters 16, 17, 23\.*

*The drowned lower halls and deep ways. Rhoswen's water rises through the flooded channel and sea caves during the climax, filling every deep way out of the covenant chamber. The hall where Edward and Amelia die is in the lower passage; their bodies lie in the flooded refuge and are never recovered. Inheritance Chapters 17, 23\.*

*The flooding servants' way. The escape route after the covenant chamber, following the map in Catherine's atlas. Water chest-deep and rising. Catherine carries Niall. Alexander goes ahead with Seraphina's lamp. Inheritance Chapter 17\.*

***From the refuge's upper halls (second assault):** The ancient door behind the relief of the crowned figures does not answer to Seraphina or to Gideon's picks. Alexander places his palm to it with the signet in his breast pocket and it opens. On the stair above, he meets Percival and steps aside rather than cutting him down. Inheritance Chapter 22\.*

### ***The Northern Shore of Loch Maree — Rowan Circle Massacre Site (1815)***

*The flat ground east of the cave entrance in the northern cliff face. This is where the Rowan Circle of Wester Ross gathered for their rites; the covenant's old carving in the tunnel behind them was known to the coven's members and was part of their lore. In the autumn of 1815 Captain Nathaniel Sorrell led hunters here and the Circle was massacred. Silas Ashcroft attended to value the spoils and catalogued the cave geometry that same night — the source of his later ability to direct Percival's surveyor to the refuge relief in Inheritance. The Heart of Morven, the Circle's binding stone, was taken by Silas from this ground. Seraphina survived because she had gone to the eastern shore to draw water and was not present when the hunters arrived. She was born within sight of this loch and has not returned to the northern shore since that night. The site has no marker.*

*The standing stones where Rhoswen begins her crossing stand on this same northern shore. When Seraphina breaks her oath at those stones in Inheritance Chapter 13, she is within yards of the ground where Anton Veress died and where her coven was destroyed thirty-seven years before. She does not say so, and no one asks. This layering — covenant ground, massacre ground, oath-breaking ground — is available to later books, particularly Book Five (Seraphina's death) and Book Seven (the gathering of the seven covenants).*

### ***The Ancient Cairn, Northern Heather***

*An ancient cairn in the heather north of the manor, its stones stacked by hands not entirely human. Its mouth is dark. Hessian reaches it within twelve minutes of emerging into the first true sunlight on 23 December 1852, well short of the thirty that would kill him. He drags himself inside and something — Josef — rises out of the dark to meet him. He begins to mummify, measured in cold, fed a little at a time by Josef's own blood. The cairn is the site of Hessian's revival across the subsequent books. Catherine's bite falls silent when Hessian enters the cairn; later books measure his return by its warmth. ## ***SCOTLAND — THE JOURNEY AND EDINBURGH***

### ***Edinburgh***

*Niall's home city and native ground. The Old Town, the Grassmarket, closes and collapsed stair — the stair from which Calum once dragged Niall. Agnes Fraser dies in Edinburgh in the winter of 1848\. On the night of her burial Calum appears across the close, and in the Grassmarket tells Niall in a few sentences what has been done to him. Niall begins sending money to the MacDuffs and to Portobello. Calum's family — a printing-house porter, a laundress, three younger siblings — still live in the Old Town. Alexander and Niall meet both families in Book Two. Series Bible; Inheritance (referenced).*

### ***Portobello (outside Edinburgh)***

*Where Niall's younger sister Isla lives with their widowed aunt, Janet Lindsay, on Niall's wages. Isla is thirteen in 1852; the Institute's file on Alasdair Fraser records only one child and does not know she exists. Series Bible.*
