## PART ONE: ENTRIES READY TO ADD

### 1. The December 1852 timeline

**Target:** Series Bible, Timeline (or a new "Book One, December 1852" block).

**Add:**
> The festival ran 27 September to 4 October 1851. Hessian bit Catherine about 19 October 1852. The sighting letter reached London 20 November. Ewan reached Sterling Castle on the night of 30 November. Webb's Inn burned on 6 December. The Dalwhinnie ford was 9 December, the gorge and the birch wood 14 December, and the party reached Loch Maree on 16 December. They entered the refuge at dusk on Sunday 19 December. The first rescue failed between about 10:30 and 11:20 that night, and Edward and Amelia died. Amelia's death came about 11:20 pm. The survivors reached the coastal bothy on Monday 20 December. Josef delivered Hessian's terms at noon on Tuesday 21 December (the solstice). Catherine walked to the manor from the croft at 1:30 am on Thursday 23 December. The ritual, the claim and the manor fire ran from about 5:15 to 6:30 am. Hessian entered the cairn at about 8:50 am. The survivors reached Sterling Castle on 31 December. The Queen received Alexander at Windsor on 11 January 1853, and Alexander wound Edward's watch on 13 January.

### 2. Sun and moon at Loch Maree, December 1852

**Target:** Location Bible, "The Northern Shore of Loch Maree" (add a note), or Series Bible, Timeline.

**Add:**
> Sunset at Loch Maree in mid-December is about 3:13 pm and sunrise about 8:43 am. The moon was new about 11 to 12 December, at first quarter on 18 to 19 December, about two thirds full on 20 December, about 78 percent on 21 December and about 90 percent on 23 December, setting at about 5:12 am that morning. At Sterling Castle on 13 January 1853 the moon is about two days old and has set by 8 pm.

### 5. Beatrice's title and history

**Target:** Main Character Bible, Beatrice, Key Formative Events and Role.

**Add:**
> Beatrice is Acting Commander from the death of Edward (19 December 1852), and is never called regent. She states it once, to Alexander's face, at 19.2. On the night of 21 December she tells him about 1839: she led an extraction off Stronsay, followed protocol to the letter, and her husband Laurence, an operative on the mission, died with a selkie mother and her two children when the authorization arrived an hour too late. Her price for her people is "a plan I can defend to the Crown," and she gives him her people, not her command. The brooch she touches is ward-worked. Who gave it is not stated.

### 6. Amelia's safeguard, read

**Target:** Series Bible, "The Northern Covenant, the Signet, and the Safeguard."

**Add:**
> Seraphina reads the safeguard by touch on 21 December (her good eye closed, her dead eye untouched): "Your mother didn't seal anything in you. She planted something." What Amelia planted is Catherine herself, the uncompelled girl, bound to the signet and the covenant. It wakes only when a Sterling claims the covenant to protect rather than to own. Seraphina can read it and cannot alter or remove it. Catherine set her terms first: Seraphina may read, and may take nothing out, change nothing, and tell no one before telling Catherine.

### 7. The claim, and what it does

**Target:** Series Bible, "The Northern Covenant, the Signet, and the Safeguard"; Location Bible, "The covenant chamber."

**Add:**
> At about 5:40 am on 23 December 1852, with his palm flat on the seal and the signet in his breast pocket, Alexander claims the covenant to protect what Hessian meant to own, his sister first. He does not draw a sword. He speaks the revocation ("By the authority this place recognizes, I revoke your welcome. You are named an enemy of Crown and Kin.") and returns Hessian's line from 16.3: "Nothing in this chamber is yours." The safeguard wakes in Catherine and her uncompelled self is restored. The pull is weakened at her refusal (23.2), no longer able to turn her after the claim, and silent from the moment Hessian is sealed in the cairn (24.4). Her bite stays cold and dark.

**5.1**

This fits the prequel and keeps the outline's cost. Logged for the bible.

**5.2**

- **Flags.** The outline calls Sebastian "fair." The Main Character Bible says "prematurely grey." **DECIDED:** fair-complexioned, hair prematurely grey. Both hold. Logged for the bible.

- **Flags.** The outline says a Sterling may be needed at the crossing, but Edward is a Sterling by birth and is already north. It does not say why Alexander is needed too. **DECIDED:** Seraphina frames it as a hazard, not a prophecy. The crossing can be made once, and guardianship is carried by blood, so if Edward is lost at the crossing another Sterling must be at hand. This foreshadows 17.x without saying it. Logged for the bible.

**7.3**

- **Flags.** The outline says this runs parallel to 7.2 but stamps it 3:30 p.m. (dusk at Loch Maree, sunset about 3:20) against 7.2's seven in the evening. **DECIDED:** treat it as one shared evening. Catherine's dusk by body clock is earlier in the day than Alexander's night, and nobody notices.

**8.1**

- **Flags.** 8.3 says Niall put the knife in Alexander's hand "an hour before." This scene is at 9 p.m. and the stair fight is at 1:10 a.m. **DECIDED:** Niall hands over the knife at the midnight watch change, off the page or in the first lines of 8.2.

**8.3**
