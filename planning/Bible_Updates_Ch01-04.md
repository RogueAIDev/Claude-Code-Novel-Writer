# Bible Updates from Scene Packets, Chapters 1 to 4
### Chronicles of the Shadow Vanguard

Decisions made while compiling the scene packets for *Inheritance* Chapters 1 to 4. Each entry gives the target document, where it goes, and the exact text to add or change.

---

## 1. Sterling Castle and invitation

**Target:** Series Bible, Part Five, "The Northern Covenant, the Signet, and the Safeguard" (add after the first paragraph). Also add a one-line cross-reference to the Location Bible's Sterling Castle entry.

**Add:**
> Sterling Castle is itself a warded hereditary holding, and its protections answer to invitation from its lawful holder. When Edward invites Hessian to dine at the 1851 Harvest Festival, he admits him as a guest for the length of his stay, and that welcome lapses when Hessian leaves the district on 1 October. To reach Catherine on the festival's closing night, Hessian needs a Sterling to open the way, and under compulsion Catherine opens her own balcony doors. The rowan-and-salt ward Seraphina gives Amelia fails for the same reason: it keeps out only what is not let in. The pattern runs from the festival to the climax, where Alexander revokes Hessian's welcome by the same law.

## 2. Silent approach

**Target:** Master Vampire Dossier, Part Five, "Concealment" (add to the end of the section).

**Add:**
> Concealment can also hide a vampire's approach. Catherine does not hear Hessian cross the long gallery at the 1851 festival, not because vampires move silently, but because his concealment makes her fail to register him. She finds it strange only later, as the effect fades from memory.

## 3. Niall and the ledger

**Target:** Main Character Bible, Niall, IV. Key Formative Events (add after the 1848 meeting).

**Add:**
> On 30 September 1851, recognizing Hessian's compulsion at work on Edward during the land sale, Niall deliberately knocks his ledger from the study windowsill so that it looks like an accident. The noise breaks the hold, and Edward refuses Harrow's Fen. Niall tells no one.

## 4. Catherine's diary lines

**Target:** Main Character Bible, Catherine, IV. Key Formative Events (add after the pin money).

**Add:**
> On the festival's closing night she writes two lines in her diary and, unlike most of her wishes, does not cross them out: "Asked Alex tonight whether he ever wishes he could simply leave. He said duty, as I knew he would." and "I am so tired of waiting rooms." Alexander reads them in October 1851 without knowing the second is Hessian's phrase.
